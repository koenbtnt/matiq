matiq Design System

The design system for matiq, the BJJ training companion, on native iOS and Android with light and dark themes. The theme follows the system setting. Exported from the design canvas (version 13).

Where this export and a canvas default differ, the canvas default with accent green wins. Other accents in the canvas Accent tweak (blue, orange, teal, gold, mono, volt) are rejected alternatives. They are not part of the system.

01 · Principles

#PrincipleMeaning
01Mat-side firstOne-thumb flows and big targets. Logging a session takes under 30 seconds, even with sweaty hands.
02Belt as languageBelt colors mean rank and nothing else, so progress reads instantly across the app.
03Quiet until it countsNeutral surfaces. The accent appears only on the primary action and on progress.
04Native at heartOne token set, with platform-native navigation, sheets, back gestures and haptics.

Theme rules

  • Accent fills keep a dark label in both themes. Accent text and lines use a darker shade on light.
  • Belts don't change with the theme. On light surfaces the white belt gets a hairline (border.strong) so it stays visible.
  • Elevation: dark steps surfaces up (base → surface → raised) and uses no shadows. Light uses white cards on an off-white base with hairlines.
  • Emerald doubles as success. Keep it clearly distinct from the kids green belt #2FA35A.

03 · Typography

Archivo, a variable font with a width axis, is used for display text, titles and numbers. Inter is used for reading and UI. Sizes are in pt (iOS), which equal sp (Android). All styles scale with Dynamic Type or the system font scale up to 200%. Display caps at 1.3×. Numbers always use tabular figures.
